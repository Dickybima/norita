Some games want you to grind. Norita wants you to slow down.

Norita is a cozy pixel farming game on Robinhood Chain. You get a little plot of land, plant seeds, watch them grow and harvest them for $NRTA. Then you plant again, a little bigger each time, until an empty field turns into a busy farm.

This article explains what Norita is, how it plays, and how to join the whitelist for early access.

## A world that never sleeps

Norita is a living pixel world. The sun rises over the hills, a farmer walks the dirt path, chickens peck by the fence and the windmill turns in the wind. When night falls, the farmhouse windows light up and fireflies drift over the fields.

The world runs on one clock for everyone:

- One day in Norita lasts 4 minutes.
- Each season lasts 7 days.
- Spring brings pink petals, summer brings long green days, fall turns the trees orange and winter covers the fields in snow.

Your crops keep growing while you're away. Come back later and they're ready to harvest.

## How to play

Norita is easy to learn. Four steps take you from an empty field to a full farm:

1. **Get your land.** Start with 6 plots of tilled soil and 20 $NRTA for your first seeds.
2. **Plant seeds.** Pick a seed from the shop and tap an empty plot, or plant every empty plot at once.
3. **Harvest.** Crops grow in real time. When they sparkle, harvest them for $NRTA and XP.
4. **Grow your farm.** Level up to unlock better seeds and buy more land, up to 12 plots.

## The crops

Every seed costs $NRTA to plant and pays back more at harvest. Bigger crops take longer and need a higher farmer level.

- **Turnip:** 5 to plant, 8 at harvest, grows in 30 seconds. Level 1.
- **Potato:** 15 to plant, 28 at harvest, grows in 2 minutes. Level 1.
- **Corn:** 40 to plant, 90 at harvest, grows in 5 minutes. Level 2.
- **Tomato:** 120 to plant, 300 at harvest, grows in 15 minutes. Level 4.
- **Pumpkin:** 400 to plant, 1,100 at harvest, grows in 1 hour. Level 6.

Every 100 XP is one farmer level. Extra land starts at 50 $NRTA and the price doubles for each new plot.

Small crops keep you busy. Big crops reward patience. The fun is in choosing what to plant next.
