How to Play

Norita is easy to learn: four steps from an empty field to a busy farm.

1

Get your land

Start with 6 plots of tilled soil and 20 NRTA to buy your first seeds.

2

Plant seeds

Pick a seed from the shop and tap an empty plot, or plant every empty plot at once.

3

Harvest

Crops grow in real time. When they sparkle, harvest them for NRTA and XP.

4

Grow your farm

Level up to unlock better seeds and buy more land, up to 12 plots.

Crops

Every seed costs NRTA to plant and pays back more at harvest. Bigger crops take longer and need a higher farmer level.

Every 100 XP is one farmer level. Extra land starts at 50 NRTA and the price doubles for each plot.

Seasons

The world of Norita never stops. The sun rises and sets, and the seasons change while your crops grow. One day lasts 4 minutes and each season lasts 7 days.

FAQ

Questions farmers ask us most.

What is Norita?

A cozy pixel farming game. You plant seeds, wait for them to grow and harvest them for NRTA, then use it to unlock better seeds and more land.

Which network does Norita use?

Robinhood Chain (chain ID 4663). Gas is paid in ETH, so you'll need a little ETH on Robinhood Chain to farm on-chain.

How do I join the whitelist?

Open the farm page and finish three steps on X: follow @noritafarm, like the pinned post and quote it. Then claim your spot.

When can I play?

Early access opens soon for whitelisted farmers. Follow @noritafarm on X for the launch date.

Which wallets work?

Any EVM wallet in your browser, such as MetaMask, Rabby, OKX Wallet, Coinbase Wallet, Trust Wallet or Phantom. On a phone, open the site inside your wallet app.

Is joining the whitelist free?

Yes. Joining only takes a follow, a like and a quote on X.
